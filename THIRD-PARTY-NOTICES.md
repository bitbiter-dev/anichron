## Time zone boundaries

The Worker looks up the time zone at a photo's position offline with
[GeoTimeZone](https://github.com/mattjohnsonpint/GeoTimeZone) (MIT), whose boundary data is derived from
[timezone-boundary-builder](https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder).

> are derived from © OpenStreetMap contributors, also under the ODbL.
